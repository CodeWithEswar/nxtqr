# NXTQR Brand Guidelines & Identity System

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

**Brand Name:** NXTQR  
**Category:** Interactive Visual Engineering Studio  
**Tagline:** Design. Visualize. Simulate. Understand.  
**Core Purpose:** A professional workspace for designing, visualizing, simulating, and understanding complex engineering systems.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Product Category Clarification:**
> NXTQR is an **Interactive Visual Engineering Studio**, **not** a QR-code generator. No QR codes, QR finder patterns, or marketing references to QR codes may appear in the brand assets, interfaces, or product documentation.

---

## 2. Visual Theme & Palette

The NXTQR brand identity is built directly on the platform's **Pure Black Monochrome** foundation.

| Token                    | Hex       | Role                                  |
| :----------------------- | :-------- | :------------------------------------ |
| **Main Canvas**          | `#000000` | Pure Black background base            |
| **Primary Foreground**   | `#FFFFFF` | Monogram body, primary wordmark       |
| **Secondary Foreground** | `#A3A3A3` | Category subtitle                     |
| **Raised Surface**       | `#080808` | Neumorphic containers, buttons, cards |
| **Elevated Surface**     | `#141414` | Dialogs, menus, elevated modals       |
| **Border**               | `#262626` | Precision boundary linework           |
| **Corner Brackets**      | `#404040` | Framed symbol                         |

---

## 3. Brand Characteristics

- **Technical & Precise:** Constructed from mathematically balanced vector paths and 6px rounded-md corners.
- **Minimal & Confident:** Stripped of unnecessary 3D gradients, glassmorphic blurs, and neon glows.
- **Developer-Focused:** High optical clarity from 16px favicons to 1200px social preview cards.
- **Scale-Invariant:** Zero raster artifacts; sharp at all standard viewport resolutions.
